# JUDGEMENT TALES — S01E01 pilot drop: premiere & realtime plan

**Produced by WANNATION MEDIA · WANNATION HOLDINGS (PTY) LTD · Reg No: 2026/463724/07**

Scope note, up front: this plan covers **format, run-of-show, promo cadence and repurposing**.
The broadcast itself, live chat moderation and any live selling are **native/human** — they happen
in the platform, run by a person. Nothing here fabricates viewer numbers, and no view/engagement
buying is part of this plan, ever.

---

## S — Scope: platform, format, goal

| | |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | **YouTube Premiere** of the 10:00 pilot (landscape 16:9). Premiere, not a bare upload: it creates a scheduled moment with a live chat, and the file becomes an evergreen, searchable, replayable asset. |
| **Secondary** | TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts vertical cut-downs (see `cut-sheets.md`), driving to the full episode. |
| **Goal** | First-episode discovery + a subscriber base for Ep 2. Success = watch-through on the pilot and comment volume, not raw views. |
| **Thresholds** | IG Live and TikTok LIVE need roughly **1,000 followers**; a new channel will not have them. A YouTube **Premiere** has no such gate, which is why it is the primary. Go-live on IG/TikTok only once the follower bar is actually cleared — check it, do not assume. |

## T — Tee up the run-of-show (Premiere, ~25 min total)

| Time | Beat | Who |
|---|---|---|
| −10 min | Premiere countdown live, chat opens. Pin the topic: *"Heaven runs on forms. Ask me anything about the queue."* | auto + host |
| 00:00 | Episode plays, 10:00 | auto |
| 10:00 | Host joins chat live for Q&A | human |
| 10:00–20:00 | 5–7 pre-written questions seeded so the Q&A never stalls | human |
| 20:00 | CTA: subscribe for **Ep 2 "Orientation Day"**, plus the one-line ask | human |

**Pre-written seed questions** (post them yourself if chat is quiet — that is normal, not failure):
1. Why is heaven's admin worse than Home Affairs?
2. Who is Brenda and should we be afraid?
3. Is Sven ever getting called?
4. What is the E-1 and why does it require itself?
5. Which accent was hardest to cast?
6. Does the Sultan's baklava actually appear in Ep 4?
7. When does Ep 2 land?

**Tech check (do it the day before, not the hour before):** upload and process the master early —
a 10-minute 1080p file needs processing time and the HD version can lag behind; subtitles must be
confirmed attached and selectable; thumbnail set; **≥10 Mbps up** if the host appears on camera at all.

## G — Go live & engage (human, native)

- Greet joiners **by name** as they arrive. This is the single highest-value live behaviour.
- Recap for late joiners every few minutes — people arrive throughout.
- Answer comments as they land; if quiet, work the seeded questions.
- **No live shopping** on this drop: there is no product, and IG's native live shopping was
  discontinued in 2023 anyway. If merch ever exists, TikTok Shop LIVE is the only place with
  real native product pins; on IG it would be a pinned comment plus keyword-to-DM.
- Capture intent with a **keyword-to-DM**: *"comment E1 and I'll send you Ep 2 the minute it lands."*
