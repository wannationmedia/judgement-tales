## Platform notes

- **YouTube Shorts:** set each Short's **Related video** to the full episode — the link appears on the Short itself.
- **TikTok / Reels:** link in bio → the episode. Pin a comment: *"Full 10-min episode → link in bio. Comment E1 and we'll DM you when Ep 2 drops."*
- **Instagram:** native live shopping is gone; capture intent with keyword-to-DM ("E1") instead.
- **Covers:** set the `covers/` image as the grid thumbnail, or the platform picks a random frame.

## Guardrails

- The satire is aimed at bureaucracy and individual characters. Keep the copy there too: no jokes
  at a faith, a people or a nation, in captions *or* replies.
- **Never** buy views, followers or engagement, and never quote numbers that didn't come from the
  platforms' own analytics.
- Measure: 3-second hold and watch-through on the shorts, click-through to the episode, average
  view duration on the episode. Native analytics only.
- The voices are synthesized placeholders. If a commenter asks, say so plainly.
