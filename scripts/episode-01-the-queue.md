## LOGLINE
Lwazi "Lucky" Nkosi dies on an ordinary Tuesday and arrives at Heaven's reception to discover eternity is run like Home Affairs. Armed with ticket **047**, he gets tangled in a black-market scheme to skip The Queue — only to learn the fast-track leads somewhere worse.

## CAST IN THIS EPISODE
- **LWAZI "LUCKY" NKOSI** — Joburg everyman, newly dead, green hoodie, backpack. POV. Deadpan SA.
- **ST. PETER** — reception clerk. Fussy old-world European. Rubber stamp as a weapon.
- **SVEN** — Viking at counter 046. Waiting since 862 AD. The queue is his whole personality.
- **NIGEL "THE BLAGGER"** — Cockney wide-boy soul. Runs favours and dodges.
- **THE SULTAN** — grand Ottoman dignitary, visiting from Downstairs. Speaks of himself in the third person.
- **BRENDA** — Heavenly HR. Clipped SA civil-servant. Buried in paperwork. The real power.
- **ANGEL RECEPTIONIST (CANDICE)** — blonde, clipboard, weaponised politeness.
- **MOSES** (cameo) — Complaints Department. Weary Persian-English. Two stone tablets.

## COLD OPEN — "THE LAST TUESDAY" (0:00–0:35)

*INT. JOBURG MINIBUS TAXI — DAY. Retro-broadcast grain. LWAZI, earbuds in, texting.*

**LWAZI** *(V.O., deadpan)*
Tuesday. Half a data bundle left. I was gonna fix my life on Wednesday.

*A gogo in the taxi hands him a R20 note. He turns to pass it forward — "Driver, change!" — the universal taxi duty.*

**LWAZI** *(V.O.)*
And then I did the most dangerous thing a man can do in this country.

*He unbuckles — there was never a buckle — and leans fully into the aisle to pass the money.*

**LWAZI** *(V.O.)*
I let go of the grab rail.

*SMASH CUT to white. A tiny comedic "boop" sound. Beat.*

**LWAZI** *(V.O.)*
Eish.

## SKIT A — "NOW SERVING: 047" (0:55–3:30)
**SETUP.** Lwazi arrives at Heavenly Reception. The queue is infinite. Bureaucracy is eternal.

*INT. HEAVENLY RECEPTION — CONTINUOUS. Marble, gold rope barriers, signage: "HEAVENLY RECEPTION", "GOOD PEOPLE GO IN". A line of the newly-dead stretches to the vanishing point. A "NOW SERVING" board reads **046** in flip-digits. ST. PETER stamps at the desk. Lwazi appears mid-step, still leaning like he's in the taxi. He straightens.*

**LWAZI**
…Sharp. Okay. So this is— *(looks up at the gates)* yoh. Okay. I'm not even mad.

**ST. PETER** *(not looking up)*
Take a ticket.

**LWAZI**
A ticket. For heaven.

**ST. PETER**
One does not simply *walk into* eternity, young man. *(rrrRIP — tears a ticket)* **Zero-four-seven.**

*Lwazi takes it. The ticket has a little halo logo and the number 047.*

**LWAZI**
And you're serving…?

**ST. PETER** *(points, deadpan)*
Forty-six.

**LWAZI**
Sharp, so it's just one—

**ST. PETER**
It has been forty-six for four hundred years.

*WIDE: the queue. Somewhere near the front, a VIKING — SVEN — stands rooted, horned helmet, arms folded, perfectly content.*

**LWAZI** *(walking the line)*
Four hundred— okay but there's like one guy in front of—

**SVEN** *(not turning)*
Do not. Skip. The queue.

**LWAZI**
I'm not skipping, my bru, I'm asking—

**SVEN**
I have held this position since the year **eight-six-two.** I have seen empires fall. I have seen the man at *forty-five* get called. *(a single proud tear)* It was a good day.

**LWAZI**
…You've been in a *line* for eleven hundred years.

**SVEN** *(finally turns, beaming)*
I am *very* good at it. *(produces a tiny laminated card)* "Queue Member of the Century." *Four times.*

**LWAZI** *(to camera, flat)*
Home Affairs has a franchise up here.

*CANDICE, the angel receptionist, glides past with a clipboard and weaponised cheer.*

**CANDICE**
Hi! Welcome to your eternal reward! Have we filled in the **E-1?**

**LWAZI**
The— what's an E-1?

**CANDICE** *(radiant)*
It's the form to *request* the form. *(hands him a clipboard thick as a Bible)* Someone will be with you shortly.

**LWAZI**
Define "shortly."

**CANDICE** *(already gliding away)*
It's a *faith*-based estimate! 😇

*Lwazi stares at the board. It flips from 046 to… 046. A tiny "no change" chime. He looks at his ticket. 047. He sits on the floor. Sven sits beside him, delighted to have company.*

**SVEN**
First millennium is the hardest.

**LWAZI** *(V.O.)*
That's when I heard about the Fast-Track.

*Push in on a shadowy figure two rows back making the universal "psst" gesture. SMASH to Skit B.*

## SKIT B — "THE FAST-TRACK" (3:30–6:45)
**ACTION / CAPER.** A black-market scheme. Overlapping *Snatch*-style patter. Everyone's running an angle.

*INT. RECEPTION — SIDE ALCOVE behind a potted palm. NIGEL "THE BLAGGER," collar up, talks fast and low.*

**NIGEL**
Right. You never saw me, I was never 'ere, I'm technically still alive — long story, admin error, love it. *(beat)* You want the Fast-Track.

**LWAZI**
I want to *know* what the Fast-Track is before I want it. That's just good sense.

**NIGEL**
Smart. I like a cautious punter. *(produces a glinting golden ticket — a halo-shaped FAST-TRACK PASS)* One of these. Skips the lot. Straight through the Pearlies. No E-1, no E-1-to-request-the-E-1, no Brenda.

**LWAZI**
Who's Brenda?

**NIGEL** *(a shudder)*
We don't say Brenda's name in the queue, bruv. *(recovers)* Now. It's not *free*, is it.

**LWAZI**
Course not.

**NIGEL**
It's a *favour economy* up here, innit. No money. God hates money, well-documented. So we trade… *good deeds.* You do me a solid, I move the pass down the line, pass reaches you, you're golden. Literally.

**LWAZI**
And the solid is…?

**NIGEL**
*(too casual)* Sven's helmet.

**LWAZI**
You want me to rob a Viking.

**NIGEL**
Borrow. The Sultan downstairs wants it for the Potluck, the Sultan gives me a chit, chit goes to the harp lady on cloud nine, harp lady owes the bloke who holds the pass, pass comes to you. *Simple.*

**LWAZI** *(to camera)*
It is the opposite of simple.

*THE SULTAN sweeps in, enormous, theatrical, two attendants.*

**THE SULTAN**
The Sultan does not *queue.* The Sultan *visits.* *(spots the helmet across the room)* …The Sultan *wants.*

**NIGEL** *(oily)*
Your Magnificence. The helmet is as good as yours. My associate here—

**LWAZI**
I'm not his associate.

**NIGEL**
—my *colleague*—

**LWAZI**
Also no.

**THE SULTAN** *(to Lwazi, grand)*
Young man. Bring the Sultan the horned hat of the northern one, and the Sultan shall grant you… *(claps; an attendant produces a baklava)* …a baklava.

**LWAZI**
I was promised a Fast-Track Pass.

**THE SULTAN**
The baklava is *extremely* good.

**NIGEL** *(whispering)*
Take the baklava as a sign of *good faith*, the pass comes after, this is how it *works*—

*QUICK MONTAGE, Snatch-style, overlapping voices: Lwazi approaches Sven; Sven talks only about queue technique; Lwazi tries to lift the helmet; Sven absent-mindedly blocks every attempt while describing the year 974; the pass changes hands three times in the background; Candice glides through narrating "faith-based estimates"; the Sultan eats a baklava.*

**SVEN** *(mid-anecdote, not noticing the theft attempts)*
…and in nine-seventy-four a man tried to skip. *One* man. *(dark)* We do not speak of what the queue did.

**LWAZI** *(hand frozen an inch from the helmet)*
…What did the queue do.

**SVEN** *(brightly)*
It *reported him to Brenda.*

*Thunder crack. The muzak stops. Everyone in the alcove freezes. The golden pass, mid-handoff, drops to the marble floor with a tiny "ting." Everyone stares at it. SMASH to Skit C.*

---

## SKIT C — "PROCESSING ERROR" (6:45–9:30)
**COMIC PAYOFF / TWIST.** Brenda arrives. The whole queue is a clerical error. The Fast-Track leads somewhere worse.

*The gold-rope barriers retract with a bureaucratic hum. BRENDA strides in, clipboard, lanyard, the posture of a woman who has never once been wrong on a form.*

**BRENDA**
*Right.* Who dropped unauthorised express documentation on a Grade-A celestial floor.

*Silence. Nigel is gone. Only his collar remains, standing upright, then wilts.*

**BRENDA** *(picks up the pass, reads it, sighs the sigh of the ages)*
A Fast-Track Pass. *(to the room)* Do you know how many of these we issued in the Renaissance? *(flips a page)* …Everyone. We issued everyone one. It was a *pilot programme.*

**LWAZI**
So it works?

**BRENDA**
Oh, it *works.* *(dead)* It fast-tracks you straight to the **Complaints Department.**

*A door we hadn't noticed creaks open. Beyond it: MOSES, at a desk, buried to the shoulders in paper, holding two stone tablets. He doesn't look up.*

**MOSES** *(weary, Persian-English)*
Complaint number one. "Water." *(flips tablet)* Complaint number two… "water, but *colder.*" *(long pause)* I have heard this one before.

**BRENDA** *(to Lwazi, almost kind)*
That's the fast-track, love. You skip the queue to reach the department that *processes the complaints about the queue.* *(beat)* Took us four centuries to build. Very proud.

**LWAZI** *(to camera, the full dawning)*
I died to escape admin. And admin followed me. *Into the afterlife.* With *better signage.*

**BRENDA**
Now. *(tears a fresh ticket)* Back of the line. Take a—

**LWAZI**
—ticket. Yebo. I know. *(takes it. Looks.)* …This one says zero-four-seven as well.

**BRENDA** *(already walking off)*
System's been down since the Reformation. We're aware.

*Lwazi returns to his spot. Sven shuffles over, pats the floor. Lwazi sits. The board flips: 046 → **047.***

**SVEN** *(gasps, grabbing Lwazi's arm, tears of joy)*
They called you. My boy. *They CALLED you.*

**LWAZI** *(standing, cautiously hopeful)*
…They called me?

*He steps toward the desk. ST. PETER slides a little sign across the counter: "**GONE TO LUNCH — BACK IN ETERNITY.**" A tiny roller-shutter comes down.*

**LWAZI** *(flat, to camera)*
Of course.

**SVEN** *(settling back, content)*
Lunch is only a few hundred years. *(offers a tiny laminated card)* Here. "Queue Member — Trainee." You've earned it.

**LWAZI** *(takes it, V.O.)*
…Eish. *(beat)* Okay. First millennium's the hardest, apparently.

*He sits. Pulls out his phone. One bar of signal. The Wi-Fi network: **"HEAVEN_GUEST"** — password required. He sighs. PULL BACK through the infinite queue as the muzak swells.*
