## EPISODE 2 — "ORIENTATION DAY" (`orientation-day`)
**Logline:** Jesus runs Heaven's corporate onboarding. Lwazi survives the slide deck, the icebreakers, and the harp mandatory-training — and just wants the Wi-Fi password.
**Cast:** Lwazi, **Jesus** (Chinese, trainer energy), **Moses** (hungover on the back row), **Pearl** (HR facilitator, teal suit, name badge), Candice.
**Art ref:** image 2 — classroom, "Orientation Day: Your New Life in Heaven", bullet slide, harp handoff.

- **Skit A — "Please Hold All Questions" (setup):** Mandatory onboarding. Jesus's slide: *Departments · Benefits · Community · The Queue · Ask Anything!* Every time Lwazi asks anything, it's "a great question, let's park it." Moses heckles from the back ("I was at the *original* orientation. There were *ten* slides. Stone.").
- **Skit B — "Icebreaker" (action):** Forced team-building. "Say your name, how you died, and a fun fact." Escalating one-upmanship between souls from everywhere (Mind-Your-Language ensemble) — a caper of social survival. Pearl hands Lwazi a harp he can't play; mandatory recital looms.
- **Skit C — "The Password" (payoff/twist):** The whole orientation exists to withhold one thing: the Wi-Fi password. It's written on a slide marked "CONFIDENTIAL." Jesus reveals the password *is* a parable. Button: the password is "Password123!" and nobody can believe it; Moses knew the whole time.

## EPISODE 3 — "THE COMPLAINTS DEPARTMENT" (`the-complaints-department`)
**Logline:** Moses has processed grievances for 4,000 years. A single new complaint — "the internet, but also too many rules" — jams the entire system.
**Cast:** **Moses** (lead this episode), Brenda, Lwazi (sent to file a complaint about the E-1), a visiting Jesus with a "process improvement" plan.
**Art ref:** image 3 — "The Complaints Department", Brenda drowning in paper, Moses with two tablets ("Complaint #1: water/bread/people/God's plan?", "Complaint #2: no internet / too many rules / telling me what to do").

- **Skit A — "Four Thousand Years of Notes" (setup):** Moses's intake ritual. Every complaint rhymes with every other complaint. He's seen it all, in stone. Lwazi arrives to complain about the E-1 and is told to take a *tablet* (a literal stone one).
- **Skit B — "Escalation" (action):** A complaint so modern it has no precedent breaks Moses's filing logic. A caper to find where new categories of grievance get filed — through HR, past the Sultan, into a storeroom of unopened suggestion boxes since the Bronze Age.
- **Skit C — "Resolved" (payoff/twist):** The fix is to mark the complaint "Resolved" without resolving it — the oldest trick in management. Moses teaches Lwazi the sacred art of "we've logged your feedback." Button: a stone tablet thuds onto the pile: "Complaint #4,001 — the complaints department."

## EPISODE 4 — "DOWNSTAIRS NEIGHBOURS" (`downstairs-neighbours`)
**Logline:** Heaven hosts a "Neighbours Potluck" with the folks from Below to improve inter-departmental relations. It all hinges on one shared elevator.
**Cast:** **The Sultan** & Ottoman dignitaries (visiting from Downstairs), Candice, the heavenly choir, Lwazi (roped into catering), Nigel (running a side-hustle on the buffet).
**Art ref:** image 4 — "Downstairs Neighbours", elevator, "Heavenly Neighbours Potluck", baklava and tagine on the table.

- **Skit A — "Mind the Gap" (setup):** The one elevator between Up and Down is being shared for the event. Strict etiquette. The Sultan "does not queue" but will, graciously, "visit." Choir vs. dignitaries' entrance music becomes a passive-aggressive battle.
- **Skit B — "The Potluck" (action):** Everyone brought the same dish. A diplomatic caper over whose baklava is canonical. Nigel runs a trade in "upstairs" and "downstairs" snacks across the rope. The joke is strictly on bureaucracy and one-upmanship — both sides equally absurd, both equally dignified.
- **Skit C — "Elevator's Out" (payoff/twist):** The elevator jams with Up and Down stuck inside together — and they get along fine until Facilities (Brenda) sends a form to "report the incident." Button: a sign goes up — "Next Potluck: pending E-1 approval."

## EPISODE 5 — "BUILD A BRIGHTER ETERNITY" (`build-a-brighter-eternity`)
**Logline:** Management launches a "transformation initiative." New branding, new mandatory harps, a "synergy" rollout nobody asked for.
**Cast:** **Pearl** & an angel middle-manager (the change-management duo), Jesus (sponsor), Lwazi (focus-group of one), Brenda (enforcing compliance).
**Art ref:** image 2's "BUILD A BRIGHTER ETERNITY" banner + the boardroom energy.

- **Skit A — "The Rollout" (setup):** A town-hall announces "Eternity 2.0." Buzzwords as scripture. The old harps are being decommissioned for "Harp Pro — now with a subscription."
- **Skit B — "Change Management" (action):** A caper to keep the beloved old harp alive, smuggling it past the compliance sweep. Everyone mouths support for the change while quietly resisting it — the universal office comedy.
- **Skit C — "Rollback" (payoff/twist):** The initiative collapses under its own paperwork; they quietly revert and call it "Eternity 2.0 (Classic)." Button: a banner drops — "Thank you for your patience during our transformation journey."

## EPISODE 6 — "THE NUMBERS" (`the-numbers`)
**Logline:** The afterlife's quarterly stats — the pie chart — don't add up. Souls are going somewhere unaccounted for, and someone has to present it to the Board.
**Cast:** **God** (Black, executive/board chair — brief, devastating cameos), the angel analyst (presenting the pie chart), the assistant taking minutes, Brenda, Lwazi (the unaccounted data point).
**Art ref:** image 5 — the boardroom pie chart (heaven / the other place / hourglass / pending), God in the white suit.

- **Skit A — "Quarterly Review" (setup):** The analyst presents the destinations pie chart. One slice — "Pending" — is quietly growing. Nobody wants to be the one to mention it to God.
- **Skit B — "The Discrepancy" (action):** A caper through the back-office to find the missing souls. They're all stuck — in The Queue. The numbers were never wrong; the *process* was. Everyone races to reassign blame before the Board meeting.
- **Skit C — "The Board Meeting" (payoff/twist):** God asks one simple question that nobody can answer. The fix: rename "Pending" to "In Transition" and change the chart colour. Button (series callback): the single largest slice turns out to be souls still filling in the E-1.
