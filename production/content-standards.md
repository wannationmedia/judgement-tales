# JUDGEMENT TALES — Content Standards & Risk Note

*Adviser's note for WANNATION MEDIA. General guidance, not legal advice; for a classification or defamation question on a specific episode, get a South African media lawyer to review the final cut.*

## The one rule that keeps the channel alive
**Punch at power, process and individuals — never at a group.** This single line is what separates protected satire from actionable hate speech and demonetizable content. It costs nothing creatively (the funniest target in this show is bureaucracy anyway) and it protects the whole venture.

## Why it matters (the three exposures)
1. **SA Equality Act (PEPUDA) & the hate-speech framework.** SA law protects satire and robust commentary but prohibits speech that is a *clear intention to be harmful or incite harm* against a group on protected grounds (religion, race, ethnicity, etc.). Reimagining sacred figures as civil servants for comedy is interpretation; asserting a group is inferior, false, or deserving of harm is not protected.
2. **Films and Publications Act / FPB classification.** Distributed online content can be subject to classification. Adult satire is fine with correct age-labelling (e.g. an age rating + content advisory on the site and uploads). Avoid anything that reads as hate propaganda or gratuitous degradation of a faith.
3. **Platform policy (YouTube/TikTok/Meta).** Their hate-speech and advertiser-friendly rules are stricter than the law. Group-attacking content gets demonetized or removed regardless of legal defensibility. Satire of institutions, management and admin is advertiser-safe.

## Do / Don't
| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Mock queues, HR, forms, KPIs, middle-management, self-importance, red tape | Claim a religion is false, stupid, or evil |
| Treat every character — incl. religious figures — with underlying dignity | Mock the act of worship, prayer, or sacred practice |
| Make the casting note explicit and visible | Imply a historical population was "really" one race as fact |
| Keep accents a source of warmth and register-comedy (Mind Your Language) | Use accents to demean a nationality or make a group the butt |
| Age-label the content (adult satire) + content advisory | Target a private real individual |

## On-site/on-video requirements
- Visible **casting & satire note** on the About/home page and episode pages (text is in `CONVENTIONS.md §2–3`).
- **Age/content advisory** on the site and on uploads (adult animated satire).
- Company credit **WANNATION MEDIA** and the legal line on every finished video.

## Episode-by-episode quick check before release
Ask of each script: *Is the joke on the bureaucracy/the individual, or on the group they belong to?* If any beat only works by demeaning a group, rewrite it so the institution is the target. The pilot already follows this — the target is The Queue, HR, and admin, not any faith.
